QuietPalate Support

For app problems, ingredient-label recognition issues, evidence-display corrections, accessibility concerns, or privacy questions, email gregk@sabasetstudios.com.

Please leave private health details out. You do not need to send a photo of your mouth or a lesion, a medical or dental record, or your full health history to report an app, label, or evidence problem.

Report template

Helpful details

  • the steps that led to the problem
  • what the app showed and what you expected instead
  • the exact printed ingredient wording for a label or evidence issue
  • the product country or region, if known
  • your device, operating-system version, and QuietPalate app version
  • a screenshot only if it helps and contains no private health information

What support can address

App or accessibility problems

Crashes, controls that do not work, unreadable layouts, and screen-reader barriers.

Label recognition

Missed, split, or incorrectly matched ingredient wording. Please include the exact words printed on the package rather than an interpretation of them.

Evidence or source corrections

A broken source link, wording that does not match a cited source, or a finding you think should be reviewed. A source link or citation is helpful when available.

What support cannot do

App support cannot diagnose a mouth change, interpret symptoms or lesion photos, or advise you to start, stop, or change a medicine or dental work. Those questions need the relevant clinician.

For sudden trouble breathing or swallowing, throat tightness, fainting, or collapse, call your local emergency number now.
