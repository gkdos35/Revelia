Accessibility

These are your phone’s settings, not ours.

There is nothing to switch on in the app. Change a setting on your phone and QuietPalate follows it.

This is the same statement shown inside the app, under Settings, then Accessibility. It is published here so it can be read without installing anything.

Larger textFollows your phone to its largest setting.
Never color aloneEvery result has a word and a shape.
Screen readersVoiceOver and TalkBack; every control named.

What the app does with them

  • Larger text. The app follows your text size all the way to the largest setting. Reading text grows in full; big page headings grow more gently, so they don’t fill the screen on their own.
  • Light and dark. The app follows whichever your phone is set to.
  • Never color alone. Every result carries a word and a shape as well as a color, so it reads the same without color vision.
  • Reduced motion. Turn it on and the app stops animating sheets and transitions.
  • Screen readers. VoiceOver and TalkBack read the app, and every control is named.
  • Buttons, not just gestures. Anything you can swipe to do, you can also do with a button.

Where to change them

iPhone and iPad: Settings, then Accessibility. Text size and contrast are under Display & Text Size, animation is under Motion, and VoiceOver has its own entry.

Android: Settings, then Accessibility.
