Support

For a problem with the app, a label it read wrongly, something in a result that looks wrong, an accessibility problem, or a privacy question, email gregk@sabasetstudios.com.

Please leave private health details out. You do not need to send a photo of your mouth or a lesion, a medical or dental record, or your full health history to report a problem with the app, a label, or a result.

Send a report

Choose whichever is easiest. The template asks only for what helps reproduce or correct the problem.

Report template

Details that help

  • the steps that led to the problem
  • what the app showed and what you expected instead
  • the exact ingredient words printed on the package, for a label
  • the restaurant and the dish, for a menu
  • your device, its operating-system version, and the QuietPalate version
  • a screenshot, only if it helps and shows no private health information

What support can help with

The app, and accessibility

Crashes, controls that do not work, layouts that cannot be read, and screen-reader barriers.

Labels and menus

An ingredient the app missed, split in two or matched to the wrong thing, or a restaurant list that no longer matches what the restaurant publishes. Send the exact printed words rather than a description of them.

Research and sources

A source link that does not open, wording that does not match the source it names, or a finding you think should be looked at again. A link or citation helps.

What support cannot do

Support cannot diagnose a change in your mouth, interpret symptoms or lesion photos, or advise you to start, stop, or change a medicine or dental work. Those questions are for your dentist, oral medicine specialist, or doctor.

For sudden trouble breathing or swallowing, throat tightness, fainting, or collapse, call your local emergency number now.

Privacy

Sending an email shares what you choose to include with your email provider and ours. When this page is opened from the app, the app version and operating-system version are added to the page address to fill in the template; the hosting provider receives that address.
