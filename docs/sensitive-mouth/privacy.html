Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 11, 2026

This notice describes the current QuietPalate mobile candidate and its official support website. QuietPalate is being developed by Sabaset Studios LLC. The mobile candidate does not require an account, use advertising, or run owner-operated health-data analytics.

No accountNo QuietPalate login or profile server.
Local recordsSaved and personal data stays encrypted on device.
No ad trackingNo advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking.
QuietPalate is still a development candidate. Privacy, regulatory, database-license, and store disclosures must be reviewed again before public app distribution.

Information stored on your device

The app may store information you choose to save, including:

  • recognised or manually entered ingredient wording
  • saved product names, label versions, barcodes, markets, and source details
  • the app's ingredient matches and evidence references for a saved formulation
  • dated observations and other personal records you choose to create
  • clinician-documented information that you choose to record without app inference
  • dental-material exposures that you choose to record
  • app settings and onboarding state

Saved formulations and personal records are encrypted and authenticated locally. The app configures its private records to be excluded from ordinary backup and device-transfer paths. These controls reduce exposure; they cannot guarantee protection against every operating-system, device-management, or forensic path.

Label photos and on-device text recognition

Camera and photo-library images are used to recognise ingredient text on the device. QuietPalate keeps only the text you review, not the label image. A camera capture or app-owned temporary copy is deleted after the review path completes on a best-effort basis; the original image you selected remains in your photo library.

QuietPalate uses Google ML Kit for on-device text recognition. Google states that the image, text, and recognition output are not sent to Google, while the SDK may contact Google for updates and send app, device, performance, configuration, event, and error metrics for diagnostics and usage analytics. Read Google's ML Kit Terms and Privacy information.

Optional barcode lookup

Barcode decoding happens on the device. Online product lookup is disabled unless a build carries the required reviewed configuration. If it is enabled and you choose barcode lookup, the retail barcode and ordinary connection metadata are sent to Open Food Facts to request product and ingredient text. Label photos and personal records are not sent with that request. Returned community data must still be checked against the package.

Open Food Facts processes the request under its own policies. See the Open Food Facts privacy page.

What Sabaset Studios does not collect through the app

In the current candidate, Sabaset Studios:

  • does not require a QuietPalate account
  • does not operate a server that receives saved formulations or personal records
  • does not provide cloud sync
  • does not include advertising SDKs
  • does not track people across apps or websites
  • does not sell personal information
  • does not receive label images, recognised text, or personal records through owner-operated analytics

Support email

If you contact support, Sabaset Studios receives the email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. That information is used to respond, investigate the report, and maintain an issue-resolution record as needed. Email is handled by the sender's and recipient's email providers under their own policies.

Please do not send mouth or lesion photos, medical or dental records, or a full health history. The Support page provides a bounded report template.

This website

These static pages do not include analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, contact forms, or embedded third-party widgets. They are hosted with GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs a visitor's IP address for security purposes when a GitHub Pages site is visited. See GitHub Pages data collection.

When you open Support from the app, QuietPalate adds its app version and the device's operating-system description to the support-page URL so the report template can be prefilled. GitHub Pages receives that requested URL as the hosting provider. The app does not add ingredient wording, label results, personal records, or health information to the support URL.

External links

The app and website may open sources, licensing information, support email, or other external services. Information sent to or collected by those services is governed by their own terms and privacy practices.

Deleting local data

Use Delete all local data in QuietPalate Settings to remove the encrypted local record and its encryption key. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data according to the device operating system's behavior. This does not delete an email you previously sent or information processed by an external service.

Security

QuietPalate uses platform-protected key storage and authenticated local encryption for saved formulations and personal records. No storage or transmission method is completely secure, so please keep your device protected and avoid sending sensitive details in support email.

Changes to this policy

This policy may change as the candidate changes or review requirements are completed. The effective date above will be updated when the policy changes materially.
