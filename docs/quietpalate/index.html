QuietPalate

An app for iPhone and Android, for people living with oral lichen planus (OLP) or oral lichenoid lesions (OLL). It reads the ingredient list on a label, or the ingredients a restaurant publishes, against published research on mouth symptoms, and keeps your own list of what bothers you and what is fine for you.

Get support Privacy notice Terms of use Accessibility

What it does

Check a label by camera, photo, barcode or typing. Look up one ingredient. See what a restaurant’s own published ingredient lists say about a dish. A name the app cannot recognize is shown as such rather than guessed at.

Private by default

There is no account. Your checks, your list and your saved labels stay on your phone, encrypted. Nothing syncs to us, and there is no advertising. The privacy notice says what a barcode lookup sends.

What it is not

QuietPalate gives information, not medical advice. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose or treat anything, and it is not a check for food allergies or celiac disease. Read the label on the package in your hand, and check with your dentist or doctor before a medical decision.
