QuietPalate

A local-first ingredient-label companion being developed with oral lichen planus in mind. It helps people inspect the exact words on a package against a bounded evidence library while keeping uncertainty visible.

Development candidate

Support Privacy Policy

What it is

A way to capture or enter ingredient wording, review what was recognised, and read source-linked information without reducing a whole product to a safety score.

Private by default

Saved formulations and personal records stay encrypted on the device. There is no QuietPalate account, cloud sync, advertising, or owner-operated health-data analytics in the current candidate.

Important limit

QuietPalate does not diagnose a mouth change, interpret lesion photos, or advise medication or dental-work changes. It is not a substitute for a dentist or doctor.
